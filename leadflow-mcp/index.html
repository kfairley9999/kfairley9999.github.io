LeadFlow MCP

An open-source MCP server that lets Claude, Cursor, or any MCP client run a shop’s front desk: capture leads, score them with a published rubric, log interactions, schedule follow-ups, and report on the pipeline.

Built for: Owners of small service businesses (plumbing, HVAC, roofing, landscaping, trades) and the AI-tooling teams who support them.

Problem & solution

Keep the call list out of loose sticky notes.

The problem

When a shop owner is on a job site, leads arrive across voicemails, texts, and web forms. Follow-ups slip, stale quotes sit for weeks, and general-purpose AI chats hallucinate priorities or lose track of dollar amounts.

How LeadFlow solves it

The host model handles conversation; LeadFlow owns the books. Every call is Zod-validated, money is stored in integer cents, and scores are computed deterministically from a published rubric—never guessed by an LLM.

Verified capabilities

What the code actually does.

11 MCP tools

  • create_lead & update_lead with idempotency keys and optimistic locking (expectedUpdatedAt)
  • get_lead & search_leads across name, service, city, email, phone, and notes
  • log_interaction across call, email, SMS, visit, or note
  • schedule_follow_up, set_follow_up_status, and list_due_follow_ups
  • pipeline_summary & formula-safe export_leads_csv

Deterministic 0–100 rubric

  • Source: referral (+22), website (+14), walk-in (+12), phone (+8), other (+3)
  • Reachability: phone (+10), email (+8), or unreachable (-12)
  • Urgency & value: high urgency (+20); job value up to +16 ($5,000+)
  • Stage & recency: proposal (+16), active ≤2d (+16), quiet >21d (-18)
  • Bands: hot (75+), warm (50+), cool (25+), cold

Resources, prompts & safety

  • Resources: leadflow://pipeline, leadflow://scoring-model, and leadflow://lead/{id}
  • Prompts: morning brief and grounded follow-up draft
  • Bearer-token guard on /mcp in Cloudflare Workers with D1
  • CSV export escapes leading =, +, -, and @ cells

  • The live demo at leadflow-mcp.kfairley9999.workers.dev uses fictional seed data (Northglass Plumbing). No production customers or live user traffic are claimed.
