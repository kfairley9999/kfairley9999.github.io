EvalsLive

llm-watch

LLM call ledger & eval regression gate

Logs every LLM call's tokens, latency, and cost to SQLite, then gates model swaps in CI with an eval suite (regex, JSON-schema, and LLM-judge scorers), a paired bootstrap 95% CI, and pass-to-fail flip detection. In the demo suite it blocks a cheaper model that causes six pass-to-fail flips.

Agents

mini-agent

Tool-calling agent with self-check & eval harness

A provider-agnostic agent loop (OpenAI-compatible: Groq, Gemini, OpenAI) with calculator, sandboxed Python, and web-search tools, a step budget, a required self-check, and rate-limit-aware retries. Scored 14/15 (93.3%) on a 15-task eval with automated checkers; the published failure analysis shows the self-check accepting an off-by-one answer, motivating a re-execution verifier.

02 — About

Reliable agents, measured honestly

I'm an AI researcher in Los Angeles focused on the unglamorous half of agentic AI: making agents measurable and dependable. I build open-source tool-calling agents and multi-agent systems, then wrap them with eval harnesses, regression gates, and code-enforced guardrails, and I publish the results, including the failures.

I design and direct my projects and build them with AI coding agents: I set the requirements, test against real models and real data, and report limits alongside results.

Before AI, I served in the U.S. Marine Corps and worked in server management, logistics, and data workflows. That background shows up in how I build: clear controls, audit trails, and systems that fail safely.
